# Ruby Heart material repair

The opaque pink body finish was rejected by the owner and removed. The private simulator now shows candidate25 with clear gemstone optics: surface reflection, refraction, internal reflection and absorption through the real faceted ruby. No gem appearance picture, fixed highlight image or diffuse pink substitute is used.

The whole ruby is traced as one continuous volume across two convex domains. The compact geometry is the repaired candidate24 geometry, byte for byte. It contains 3 meshes and 17980 triangles in a 444279 byte USDZ. The original detailed model and editable full and compact Blender checkpoints are preserved. The native detailed versus compact comparison passed on candidate24; candidate25 changes only uniform optical color parameters. That earlier comparison is geometry evidence, not a claim of a new candidate25 baseline capture.

The actual candidate25 model bytes and visible Ruby Heart heading were verified in the private simulator. Both optical domains and both reflections attach successfully. Six native poses and nine distinct frames from a complete turn were inspected. The ruby retains its body color, with changing internal reflections. Every outer edge was inspected in nearest neighbor crops at four times size. Internal bright facets remain; no new broken outer outline or geometry hole was seen.

The uniform absorption is red 0.042528659, green 9 and blue 0.721236937, with optical density 0.5 and exposure 0.681357060. These are spatially constant optical parameters. The studio and other gem profiles remain unchanged. The runtime snapshots saved in this run are required for the custom preview optics; a USDZ alone does not include the runtime shader.

The front still differs visibly from the reference in facet layout and internal reflections. The back has broader pale facets than the reference front. The whole badge is not accepted as complete. A subsequent reference crown geometry experiment failed strict numerical checks before Blender import or native installation and was not adopted. The shader tolerance was not weakened.

See review/reference_comparison.png, review/motion_review.png, review/native_gem_validation.json and review/motion_validation.json. Ruby Heart is left at its resting front in Badge Studio Preview. Production app files remain untouched. The three crowns remain held by the owner, and Ocean Navigator candidate14 is preserved unfinished.
