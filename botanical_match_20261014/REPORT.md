# Sunrise and Emerald sprout

Both use the new broad flat raised rim and recessed face. Their outer diameter, floor cut, identity transforms and app casing/material names are preserved. Existing installed badges and the shipped silver source were untouched.

Sunrise: the old road trace put its inner bend about 50 reference pixels too far inward. Gold road pixels outside the narrow mesh then appeared on the recessed background, making an extra curve. The trace now follows the reference edge, the road is one native Blender beveled plate, and its foreground/background maps were refitted against a fresh Home calibration. The old radial squeeze of lower outline points was also removed. The finish is copper, including the side wall. Final: 36,218 triangles, 2,992,831 bytes, SHA-256 `28e041d0602547a4fe3265642291b40a4b553c6c028348949e9677aa798060b1`.

Sprout: leaf borders and veins are thicker; the raised borders are continuous at a controlled height instead of following unreliable facet-edge ray hits. Vein heights are smoothed, the stem is beveled, and the soil border ends inside the recessed face rather than extending onto the new rim. Reference-fitted maps give richer emerald and brown, with gold-only continuation where wider borders cover green reference pixels. Final: 28,008 triangles, 2,819,947 bytes, SHA-256 `1423feb3e380744c065e02e7544d5d5e66126b0593458b3ebe2f1bce2388cc0b`.

Complete coins were authored and inspected in live Blender, then checked in the actual Home renderer at rest, from the back and both sides, and in scroll recordings. The final frames also received 4x nearest-neighbor checks. Materials use fitted color/emission plus real relief geometry; they are not refraction simulations. The fixed app lighting, backdrop and floor reflection remain. Fine reference grain is softened for phone resolution. These are closer working results, not a claim of pixel-identical reproduction.

`usdchecker --arkit`: `Success!` for both models. Per-model `validation.json` proves frame/floor bounds, identity transforms, resolvable textures, the matching authorized shell, no shell openings except the floor cut, and the untouched original silver checksum. Final `ScreenTime-UIOnly` private-simulator builds returned `SUCCEEDED`: Sunrise 26,039 ms, Sprout 144,309 ms. Structured build logs are alongside this report. The production three-scheme/test gate was not run for these external model previews.

Tests used `/tmp/achievement_coin_app_20261014` and the separate `Badge Coins Preview` simulator (`85DF6D3B-6622-491A-8996-3ED0725DB273`). Each temporary Silver dataset replacement was restored byte for byte immediately after its build. No production app code was edited, no commit was made and no branch was switched.

Each model's editable `.blend`, canonical `reliefs.npz`, `design.json`, fitted `textures/`, and `mobile/` USDZ are retained. `pack.py <name>` repackages those saved sources without rerunning the tracing or fitting. Recutting geometry uses `prepare_details.py`, `correct_road_trace.py`, and `trim_soil_border.py`, then the live Blender builders and a new app calibration. The owner-facing deliverable is one compact picture, `~/Downloads/Coin badge previews.png`.
