3. Arches: free standing round tubes off the velvet, rising vertically and turning at 69% of H; thick outer and thin inner rails, satin engraved channel, 4 to 5 faceted diamonds and 2 to 3 gold framed rubies each, crest knob clusters at the bend.
4. Velvet: about 10 vertical pleats, lumpy domed top, matte crimson with rose crests and near black recesses.
