# Approved coin standard

The owner approved Emerald Sprout candidate03 and requested the same template on every matching coin. All 12 are now converted and installed in the separate simulator preview, with a rim 20 percent narrower from the outside and a complete body 30 percent thinner than the original.

The final family files, reusable shell template, Blender checkpoints, simulator captures and validation are documented in [the family report](family/REPORT.md). Earlier Sprout trials and original models are preserved. Production app files remain untouched.
