**How the reflection becomes blurry and disappears**

```text
Texture Coordinate: Window
```

```text
contactY = 0.34394771
endY     = 0.18422583

t = clamp((contactY - windowY) / (contactY - endY), 0, 1)
```

Those endpoints span approximately **230 pixels** in the 1440-pixel-high render.

```text
roughness = sqrt(
```


The floor stays opaque. The apparent reflection opacity comes from mixing its reflective material into black.

```text
rearFade = 1 - smoothstep(-5, -1, worldPosition.z)
```
