RADIANT MOUNTAIN SUNBURST CHECKLIST (percent of height down from the top tip, percent of width in from the left spike tip)

OVERALL
* Round sunburst disc on top with 5 gold spikes (top, upper left, upper right, left, right); white cloud bulges lower left and right; concave curves pinch to one sharp point at the bottom center. Mirror symmetric.
* Width about 0.98 of height; body without spikes about 0.94 of height; ring outer diameter about 0.82 of height.
* Sections down: spike tip above ring 7%; ring top to sun top 23%; sun top to central peak 10%; peak to cloud tops 20%; cloud band 24%; below clouds to bottom tip 15%.
* Landmarks: diagonal spike tips 17% down and 14% in; central peak 40% down at center; side spike tips 46% down; ring and sun center 48% down; ring disappears behind clouds at 61%; clouds widest at 71%; star gem center 81%; bottom tip 100%.
* Spikes stick out past the ring: side about 8% of W, top about 7% of H, diagonal about 6% of W. Bottom point a sharp V of 90 to 100 degrees. A small concave notch each side at 61% where ring meets cloud bulge.

FINISH AND LAYERS
* Gold: polished warm yellow gold, near white highlights, orange brown shadows, rounded or beveled, standing above the enamel.
* Enamel: smooth, glossy, slightly domed, soft lighter band inside each gold line, no grain. Every field separated by raised gold lines about 1% of W (cloisonne).
* Front to back: star gem and frame; clouds and outer border; central mountain; side mountains; sun and rim; spikes and rays; outer ring; red background.

SUNBURST
* Outer ring: gold band about 2.5% of W, visible from 20 degrees below horizontal left over the top to the right, stepped profile (wide rounded band, dark groove, thin inner lip); spikes pass over it.
* Red wedges: 10 visible triangles, vermilion red orange (about #D8502C) with a lighter salmon edge, glossy.
* Top spike: long narrow triangle about 30% of H long, base about 12% of W; yellow enamel between gold edges inside the ring (lemon near ring, yellow orange near sun), solid polished gold point with center ridge above the ring.
* Diagonal spikes (2) at 40 to 45 degrees, about 29% of W long: a solid gold point beyond the ring, and inside the ring a lemon yellow kite with thick gold edges widening toward the sun (corrected Nov 8, 2026 by sampling the mockup).
* Side spikes (2) horizontal, about 31% of W long: a solid gold point beyond the ring, and inside the ring a lemon yellow kite with thick gold edges widening toward the sun (corrected Nov 8, 2026).
* Yellow inside the spikes: the lemon kites of the five spikes (#FFC30F deepening to #F5A000 near the sun); there are no separate flanks (corrected Nov 8, 2026).
* Short rays: 4 thin yellow slivers outlined in gold splitting red sectors, from sun rim to the ring's inner lip, about 18% of W long, base 3% of W.
* Sun disc: circle about 37% of W across, upper 55% visible, glossy domed golden yellow (#FFC21A) grading to deep orange (#F07D10) lower left.
* Sun rim: gold ring about 2% of W with a thin bright inner lip.

MOUNTAINS
* Central mountain: peak 40% down at center, about 29% of H tall and 51% of W wide, slopes about 42 degrees, gold outlined.
* Central snow cap: upper 45%, two flat facets (left bright white, right pale grey lavender #CFCCD8), jagged gold outlined snowline with 3 to 4 teeth.
* Central lower faces: teal blue (#2D7DAA) glossy, right side darker steel blue, deep navy band (#1A3478) lower left; one gold outlined navy chevron sub peak right of center.
* Side mountains: smaller peaks at about 29% and 75% from the left, 48% down, about 18% of H tall and 25% of W wide, snow caps on the upper 40% with two facets and a zigzag snowline, deep royal navy (#1F3B88) bodies.

CLOUDS AND BORDER
* Cloud masses lower left and right (each about 37% of W by 28% of H), warm off white (#F2EEE9) glossy enamel with faint grey lavender shade near the gold.
* Each side: big outer lobe forming the widest point at 71% down; smaller inner lobe; thick gold curl about 8% of W rising from the lower border and curling up and inward with a tapered tip; a white lower band narrowing to the bottom tip.
* Outer border: thick polished gold band about 3.5% of W tracing the whole lower outline, stepped (wide rounded band, groove, thinner inner band), dark bronze wall at the bottom edge.

STAR GEM
* Star frame: 4 pointed gold star with concave sides centered at 81% down, about 30% of W wide and 29% of H tall, band about 3% of W beveled with a ridge, sharp points; top point overlaps the mountain base, lower arm merges with the bottom tip.
* Gem: 4 pointed crystal star with concave sides, about 19% of W by 21% of H, 8 flat triangular facets (ridges to the tips, valleys to the inner corners), pale cyan to white upper left, sky cyan (#3CC0F0) middle, deep azure (#1675C0) lower right, white sparkle near the center, crisp facet edges.

DETAILS
