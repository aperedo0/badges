# Full chest material rollout

Current complete native checkpoint: master.blend. Complete starting checkpoint: baseline.blend. The original 14_edge_review checkpoint remains preserved. The owner authorized full wood and structural iron testing on November 17. Reference imagery supplies artistic direction rather than an exact reproduction target. Stop after overall evaluation.

The body and lid envelopes receive coordinated wood charts by construction. The approved front lower sculpted plank retains its material, UVs and geometry exactly. Four shared atlas variants are derived from its existing authored maps and height data. Three are knot free; the rare knot variant is reserved for the rear lower board. Side and end grain follow the board length. Seven lid stave charts use ellipse arc length, with independent chart offsets and scale. Interiors and undersides are included. No mirrored UV variants are used.

Thirty further structural iron objects receive one shared copy of the existing layered shader. The existing lower rim and its two iron knobs remain exact. Local convex bevel wear replaces masks authored specifically for the test rail. The original coarse hammer layer is enabled as bump on the new unchanged meshes, which have no preexisting sculpted hammer field. Existing weather, roughness, fine marks and metallic layers share the original images. Curved hoops use separate arc and side charts, belts use perimeter charts, and posts and boots use surface plane charts. The material distinctions are explicit in assignments.json. Decorative lock, handles, hinges, rivets and ornaments retain their original placeholders.

All mesh coordinates, polygon topology, normals, transforms and functional hierarchy are preserved. New surfaces use full tangent normal or bump relief for this material rollout; the original benchmark components retain their physical relief. This stage does not add sculpted grain or edge chips to the new components.

The isolated Full chest material evaluation scene uses the existing warm review lighting. Its camera frames the complete chest, its floor is moved into contact for presentation, and its camera background is separated from environmental reflections. Test plank master retains the source camera, lights and world. The original reference editor and source complete scene are preserved.

prepare_wood.py caches the coordinated atlas. build_and_validate.py builds a working copy from baseline and verifies geometry, benchmark preservation, decorative materials, old UVs and new large triangle UV validity. Rebuilding replaces master.blend, so preserve any later owner edits first. apply_live.py loads a verified background result only from the known untouched previous checkpoint and preserves the current UI. Render with CHEST_FINAL=1 and render_batch.py full close. Baseline files and all required production maps are packed in the complete native checkpoints.

Mapping corrections: the upper lid frame is one welded mesh, so connected components alone cannot distinguish its curved straps. Roof position and actual band locations define complete hoop charts. Inner and side faces need their own radial coordinates. A few nonplanar corner faces need direct plane projection; the repair and validation tolerances agree. Detailed charts and verification remain in assignments.json and validation.json. No additional software or external assets were used.
