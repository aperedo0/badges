Opening mechanism design notes to complete and review before modeling

These are saved working dimensions, not an approved plan. Keep the reference exterior and existing successful frame. New moving mechanical parts should be constructed after the common height conversion, so pins and bores are circular around the actual rigid animation axes rather than being squashed again by the 0.95 height correction.

Hasp and receiver
Keep a true fixed pin through interleaved fixed and moving knuckles at the front hasp hinge. The lid carries its fixed mounting leaf and pin, while a central sleeve belongs to the swinging hasp. Preserve the broad curled strap shape, expose the stepped barrel ends and narrow the strap to about 0.455 as the reference review requested. The returned hasp tip enters a real receiver pocket on the body mounted thick shield case. It needs a cross bore across X for a retracting pin, centered on the returned tip around local Y negative 0.025 and Z 1.970 before any coordinate conversion. A circular pin radius about 0.028 fits a 0.110 thick tongue with real wall margin. Exact post conversion dimensions and the complete extraction arc must be measured before approving this design.

Retaining bolt concept
For the proposed 0.455 wide hasp tip, a pin spanning X negative 0.340 to positive 0.290 in the locked state can retract 0.540 to span negative 0.880 to negative 0.250, clearing the tip by about 0.0225. It fits inside a backing case roughly X magnitude 0.940. Use an actual receiver bore, pin guide and retained sliding carrier rather than animate solid parts through one another. Make a thicker front and back case with a real interior chamber, preserving the shield silhouette, five face fasteners and its two shoulder fasteners.

Key, cam and linkage concept
A key turns a cam about the existing keyhole axis. A radius 0.270 crank pin drives a vertical slot on the bolt carrier: X motion over a 180 degree turn is negative 0.540 from the initial locked position. A second 180 degrees returns the bolt after the hasp is clear, putting the key back into its removable orientation. The crank slot must cover the full pin Y travel, with real running clearance and no carrier collision. Key shaft and bit must pass through the actual key opening before turning in the interior chamber. This is a simplified physical warded animation mechanism, not a manufacturing ready security lock.

Opening order
Insert key, turn 180 degrees and retract bolt, swing hasp outward and up about its X axis, turn key the remaining 180 degrees to return the bolt in the empty receiver, withdraw key, then lift the lid. Reverse the sequence for a mechanically coherent closing demonstration. A bolt driver based on cosine of the key angle keeps its actual cam pin in the carrier slot. Use separate controls for key insertion, key turn, hasp angle and lid angle, with markers and a saved demonstration action. The current rest state should show no key, preserving the reference view.

Hinges and motion
A rear X hinge axis near original Y 0.521 and Z negative 1.940 sits outside the rear metal, so an outward lid flange can clear the body while opening. Start with about 100 degrees of opening and a front hasp angle around 150 degrees outward, then verify both by actual intersection sweeps. Lid ownership includes all hoops, arches, crown tabs, armor, roof heads and the fixed hasp support. Body ownership includes lower frame, handles, feet, shield case and retaining bolt. Hasp ownership includes only its sleeve, strap, edge rails and returned tip. Each rear hinge needs interleaved leaves on the correct side of the seam and a common pin.

Interior and rig
Hollow body and barrel lid while preserving the exterior. A closed hollow shell has complete interior walls and a real opening; a solid box with the lid moved away is unfinished. Plan body floor, wall and curved lid thickness and edge rabbets. Rigid bones or equivalent named rigid controls must preserve closed rest geometry and transform ownership. Reset parenting and animation safely before rebuilds. Validate whole motion and several partial angles with explicit allowed contacts; a few stills do not prove clearance. Keep a part manifest and collision report. Static closed mobile constraints still apply; the animated open envelope may require future app framing and must be reported separately. No app code or export claim until that later integration.
