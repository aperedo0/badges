Attachment defects

1. The bottom center plate rivet crowds the lower edge. Raise it about one head diameter.
2. Both handle mounting rivets crowd the top and side edges. Lower about one head diameter and inset about half a diameter.
3. Lid opening widens into triangular dark notches at armor and rail joins. Make it continuous and about half the widest current gap.
4. The lower handle sleeve swells unevenly. Use a consistent diameter and distinct shallow end bands.

No torn surfaces, major jagged geometry or obvious pinched face normals were observed. Still pictures do not establish freedom from flicker.

Required geometry corrections

1. Upper corner armor needs a narrow real perimeter bevel, about one tenth of plate face height.
2. Reduce foot shoulder height about one quarter and extend the main face upward. Raise foot rivets about one sixth of its main face height.
3. Widen the hanging strap about 10 percent. Its exposed side layers look too prominent.
4. Simplify barrel ends to two rounded collar groups and halve groove prominence.
5. Widen the crown tab about 20 percent, soften its lower corners and close the black slit beside its fold to a fine contact seam.
6. Round the handle cover end and lower transition about twice as much.
7. Shorten the handle oval about 10 to 15 percent while keeping its width.
8. Narrow the lock shield bevel about 15 to 20 percent.
9. Give the large bottom center rivet a clear plate margin.

Rivet counts broadly match the actual reference: denser inner bands, sparse outer bands, three end arch heads including the tab, upper and lower rails, paired corner faces, two handle mounting heads, two large lock shoulder heads and five small shield heads. No missing count correction is supported. Placement corrections remain at the handle plates, feet and center plate.
