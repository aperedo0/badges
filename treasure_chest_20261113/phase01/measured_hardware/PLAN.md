7. Risks and checks
Flat picture substitute: all pieces are closed solids with actual relief; a side view would reject a projected card. Grid fuzz: only boundary, bevel and profile rows are used; a triangle overlay would reject a raster grid. Wrong parts and proportions: traced_part_outlines.png and matched face close views compare the five actual reference regions; wrong generic caps must fail this check. Tiny or lost features: inspect at actual development size, 2x whole coverage and 4x hardware; final small phone check remains later.
Normals and facets: keep broad faces flat, curved sections smooth and split at intended ridges; exact dome gradients and enlarged render inspection catch pinches or mushy edges. Grain, copied lighting and color wash are prevented here by neutral untextured materials; final metal, color, glow and grazing optics are explicitly later gates. Closedness, winding and normals are checked on every operand and union result. Back and side continuity are checked in the six rendered views.
Specific risks: (1) independent measured planes disagree at the fold: reconcile to the fixed corner and common top/bottom before meshing; raw calibration preserves the discrepancy. (2) polygon offset consumes the foot notch: all four stored 0.050 offsets are valid and contained by their outlines; reject any topology change or invalid offset. (3) a Boolean retains an internal cap or creates slivers: check welded edge incidence, positive volume, duplicate triangles and actual oblique renders; keep the source checkpoint. (4) bmesh clamps away the perimeter bevel: explicit stored rings own its 0.050 width, so the modifier cannot silently reduce it. (5) the corner overhang is erased by a uniform seam rule: compare the measured stepped corner joint, while rejecting accidental open triangular gaps. (6) the cover Boolean consumes its thin lower return: inspect cross section and actual ring clearance before acceptance; a missing wall fails. (7) the latch borders self intersect: positive inner radius, triangle intersection inspection at the bend and oblique close views own this. (8) mirrored object transforms shift the spin axis: bake coordinates and prove identity matrices, exact bounds and full turning envelope.
Catalog sections opened: traced outlines, layered parts and symmetry. Enamel, bright saturated color, gems, transparent glass, fine finish marks and coin shell are outside this neutral hardware pass. Layer crossings and overlaps are specified above, with no coplanar render shells. Whole finish coverage prevents sampling only favorable areas. Reflection, final bloom, texture resolution, material names and performance remain original final phase checks rather than being claimed satisfied now.
