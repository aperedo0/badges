Risks and failing checks
1. Two layers remain: assert the old outer hoop is inactive and one active connected arch has both surfaces. A separate coincident outer strip fails.
2. Faceted or striped curved metal: analytic normals split at cross section corners. Whole 2x images and a 4x arch crop catch triangle bands or paper creases.
3. Curved cap triangles cross the elbow: constrained clean cross section tessellation, closed edge counts, nonzero triangle area and positive volume catch missing, overlapping or flipped surfaces.
4. Crown tab floats: sample the real arch surface at top and face, require 0.002 buried contact over the intended seat, then inspect a side lit crown render. A gap hidden from front fails.
5. Tab ends early or has a flat bottom: compare its lower contour with the same radial offset curve and inspect a matched end close view. Bevels cannot excuse a short tab.
6. Head floats or pierces: test its buried back against actual arch or tab face and inspect the dome from both sides.
7. New intersections at the rail: keep bottom ends inside the existing upper rail depth and inspect both lower arch ends in front and rear. A rectangular ledge remains unfinished.
8. Bounds or normals compound: immutable core reset, one height conversion, identity transforms and the full closed turn envelope. A second scale or independent shrink fails.
9. Triangle cost grows: report all active meshes and triangles. Use 128 arch intervals and small profile arcs, not a dense grid. No final phone performance claim. Current detailed source is 126996 triangles and final optimization is a later finished phase.
10. Approved parts regress: compare complete vertex arrays for unaffected parts with candidate18 and render full front, ends and back. No changed lower seam, foot, diamond position, upper rail height or nesting is accepted.
11. Finish defects get ignored for a score: fresh review first lists surface defects, then judges the scoped shapes. Whole chest acceptance remains open. A bad crown surface cannot pass on silhouette alone.
12. Runtime and pacing: check schedule before modeling and renders, save before each long operation, declare finite busy time and finish within the work deadline. No extra modeling during break. The existing timer owns the wake.
