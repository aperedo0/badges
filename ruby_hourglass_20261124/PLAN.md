## Checks and failure modes

* Front silhouette and 40 point reduction: compare model masks to source row widths and drawn contours. A pin waist, V shaped base or hard square rail tip fails. Rails, stone and gaps must remain individually legible at rest. Compare upper and lower separately. Overlay and inspect negative spaces at 4x; a missing rail must fail the profile.
* Validate each piece individually, positive volume, all edges paired with opposite direction, no degenerate or duplicate triangles. Check mirrored coordinates front and back. A one-sided cap, inside out rail or bad normals fails. Both hulls must pass native plane extraction, including mirror transforms used by the renderer; verify ruby attachments on each and reflection. A fallback opaque pink slab fails.
* Rest, early turn and grazing angles: inspect broad facets for a dead rectangular patch or lone white pin flash; change actual cut or crown depth if the center stays black or pink as it turns. No draw through or zipper at the waist. Inspect source Z intersections and crops. Inspect the top edge and lower extreme at 4x for halos and aliasing. A continuous bright face is allowed; broken specks are not.
* Side, back at half size and opposite side: look for flat slivers instead of a deep gem, striations on polished planes, a vanished bronze rail at unexpected angles, square cut ends, shadows painted on the back and stitching along the rail elbow. True mirrored crowns and rounded rails address these. At exactly 90 degrees left and right rails can coincide by symmetry. Check adjacent angles as well.
* Reflection: check a clean gap at shared floating height, no bright dots on its lowest edge, no coin contact geometry or baked stage. Symmetry and lighting are independent: a literal mirrored texture would give identical highlights on both bars and fails.
* Features in the catalog: traced proportions, saturated ruby, saturated glow risk, bilateral symmetry, real gems, thin polished metal, glass, fine brushing and adjacent solids. Check actual picture at native pixels in addition to measurements, check AgX and app lighting, no z fighting along waist, inspect local contact at all joins. No enamel fields, gold borders, coin wall, crossing motifs, decals or independent perforations. At small size the internal ridges simplify but the double triangle and outside rails survive.
