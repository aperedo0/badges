Fresh review of Emerald Stag version 1 (badge-reviewer), November 8, 2026.

CLOSENESS: 48

TOP FIXES:
1. Stag: champagne gold satin; body about 9% of H deep with deep chest; upright neck about 9% of W with mane ridges; wedge head with eye and ear; raised gold ridge panels; slender knobby legs with hooves.
2. Antlers attached to the head with burrs: two beams about 2% of W sweeping back to x 33%, the upper rising into a C to (37%, 9%), the lower hooking down to (33%, 21%); 8 to 10 curved hooked tines; saturated orange gold.
3. Base as a shallow chevron bowl: no vertical end walls; widths at 80% and 90% of H 0.56 and 0.23; one concave rim about 3% of H with pointed corner tips; dark forest marble with thin branching gold cracks; thicker middle strip and bottom band; keel ridge.
4. All gold about 3 times thicker, rich polished gold; trunk an inward curving twisted branch 2.5% of W tapering to 1% with 6 to 8 twigs; stems for the lower left and right sprigs so nothing floats.
5. Angular faceted dark marble rock; moss as raised fuzzy mounds; deep jade two tone leaves with gold midribs; ivory cream cup flowers with gold edges and stamens.

Also: center emerald 1.4 times bigger, double stepped bezel; side emeralds a third darker; no white flashes on stones; ladder like stripes on the base end face in the right side view.
