# Emerald Trophy exact arrow background

Candidate07 copies the complete green background from the final Emerald Archer USDZ. The full `New_field` material graph, `Front_field` and `Back_field` meshes, and both field PNG files are copied directly from the saved arrow model. This includes every authored color value, metallic and roughness input, texture connection, sampling setting, UV, point, triangle and normal. `candidate07/background_validation.json` records the exact copy checks. It removes the remaining standalone diffuse color difference and the tiny normal rounding differences left by the earlier separate construction.

The native packer verifies that every prim outside those three background branches is unchanged from candidate06, and that all packed texture bytes are unchanged. The matched gold rim, reconstructed cup, leaves, flames, ribbon, emerald detail, side walls, floating placement, studio lighting, floor, reflection, glow and gem shader remain intact. The green surface and material are exact shared assets. This does not assert that whole composite screenshots containing different gold artwork are identical, or that the earlier inactive color value was the cause of the owner's perceived difference.

The complete trophy was loaded into live Blender and its visible front and back fields replaced by the complete saved arrow field objects and material. The new editable scene was saved with packed maps and inspected at the front, back, both sides and an angle. The live command's connection timed out during the large import and save, but the operation finished; a follow up verified the saved file, active scene and both copied field objects. The native USDZ is copied directly from the final arrow USD, avoiding any Blender conversion differences in the background.

The separate preview build succeeded with the existing FocusSessionManager unreachable code warning. Native app inspection includes the front, back, both sides, full turn, reflection and four outline enlargements at 4x. No new broken outer outline, detached bright outer specks or external bloom puffs were observed in these checks. The simulator gesture helper failed once during initialization and then recovered for the pose capture. Apple ARKit USD validation and all local app contract checks pass, including the closed shell, complete field coverage, identity transforms, Y up meters, matching back, and no degenerate triangles, zero normals, lights or cameras.

The selected USDZ is `mobile/emerald_trophy_badge_mobile.usdz`, and the full editable scene is `emerald_trophy.blend`. Earlier final files remain in `previous/`. `background_comparison.jpg` places the arrow and trophy native app captures together at equal coin diameters. `comparison.jpg` compares the trophy with its original reference. Candidate07 contains all pose captures and the full turn recording.

The file is 10,711,415 bytes with 154,724 triangles. The size and triangle guideline exceptions are inherited from the detailed double sided traced sculpture, with their rationale preserved in the earlier reports. Physical device performance has not been measured. Floating placement remains size 0.96 and lift 0.56, with lowest world Y minus 2.455. The final hash is in `validation.json`.

The result is selected facing forward in Badge Studio on Badge Studio Preview, simulator `DF439C70-9FD7-4EF7-998E-43AF5F83A588`. Only the private preview asset was replaced; production app code and assets were not written. No commits or branch changes were made.
