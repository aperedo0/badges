# Emerald Trophy refinement

The selected model is candidate04. The owner identified small differences from the mockup and asked for the arrow badge's green background. The revision copies Emerald Archer's saved field color and roughness maps byte for byte, with the same metallic value, UV scale, and shallow curved field. `candidate04/arrow_field_match.json` records the hashes. The arrow and trophy were both inspected under the same simulator lighting.

The cup now has a rounded lower shoulder, fuller curled handles, a continuous turned mouth collar, and broader chased acanthus leaves with a central fleur de lis. The ribbon follows the reference's broad upper and lower edges, with curved hems and a rolled reverse. Torch tongues have broader joined bases, laurel leaves have more surface divisions and three pairs of veins, and the two emerald crowns each have forty facets with an ordered light and dark arrangement. Both faces carry the complete matching relief.

The complete previous trophy was loaded into live Blender as the working context. The finished front, back, both sides, and angled relief were inspected through the live connection. After the first app inspection, uneven highlights on the stacked mouth bands were corrected by replacing them with one continuous collar. The separate preview was rebuilt and checked again. Blender captures, native app captures, the full turn recording, carving enlargement, and four outline crops are in `candidate04/`.

`comparison.jpg` shows the reference and the revised native app result at equal outer coin diameters. `refinement.jpg` shows the previous and revised app views at the same size. `mobile/emerald_trophy_badge_mobile.usdz` is the selected file and `emerald_trophy.blend` is the complete editable scene. The previous delivered model and scene are preserved in `previous/`, with all earlier candidates retained.

The approved complete gold shell, geometry, normals, UV sets, material graphs, and texture bytes are unchanged. Its runtime brushing, compact inner flashes, and glint shield are preserved. Studio lighting, camera, floor, reflection, and the gem shader are unchanged. No production app files were written. Only the model asset in the separate Badge Studio preview was replaced.

The model passes Apple's ARKit USD checker and the local app contract validator. It uses meters, Y up, identity transforms, a closed complete circular shell, and no cameras or lights. Bounds are X and Y from minus 3.015 to 3.015, Z from minus 0.783 to 0.453. The exact matching back is a Y half turn of the front. No zero normals or degenerate triangles were found. Floating placement remains size 0.96 and lift 0.56, with lowest world Y minus 2.455.

The model is 7,360,289 bytes and 128,844 triangles, using 18 combined relief meshes plus the shell. The size and triangle guidelines are exceeded to retain finer leaf veins, continuous cup carving, smooth banner folds, and detailed relief on both faces. Maps are 1024 pixels, copied from the arrow. Simulator loading completed and the model turned correctly; physical device performance has not been measured.

Native Home captures were inspected from the front, both sides, and the back. The outer outlines were reviewed at 4x. No new detached outer bright specks, broken shell outline, or external bloom puffs were observed in those captures. Fine carving and hems still reduce to a few pixels at Home size. The rim remains broader than the mockup because the hard shell preservation rule was retained. The fixed app light produces a different highlight pattern from the reference. The flame shapes, acanthus carving, and banner returns remain modeled interpretations rather than an exact reconstruction.

The private project is `preview/ScreenTime.xcodeproj`, scheme `ScreenTime-UIOnly`, bundle `com.antonioperedo.badgestudio.preview`, running on Badge Studio Preview. The final build succeeded with the existing FocusSessionManager unreachable code warning. No logic tests were added for this presentation change. The revised trophy is left selected facing forward. No commits or branch changes were made.
