# Emerald Trophy reference reconstruction

Candidate05 replaces the generic repeated shapes that the owner rejected. Every visible gold leaf tip, upper sprig, berry, flame silhouette, cup contour, handle curl and plinth outline is traced from the reference image. The broad ribbon and its loose returns are traced as separate overlapping sheet regions. The sculpture has curved relief depth, a rounded cup body and stem, fluted torch depth, continuous side walls, and two gems with explicit flat triangular facets. The complete back mirrors the front sculpture.

Fine leaf veins, flame channels, cup carving, ribbon creases and the exact emerald surface pattern come from the owner's image in 1024 pixel material maps. A neutral app pass measures the light on the actual relief. `solve_reference.py` inverts the app's AgX transform and solves the material maps. The initial physical fit washed out the gold and duplicated reference highlights. The selected finish removes the neutral dielectric reflection floor and retains a small live metal contribution, with most surface tone provided by reference driven emissive fill. This intentionally prioritizes the owner's request for reference fidelity over the guidance favoring all highlights from runtime light. The shell and side walls still respond fully to the original studio lighting. Fine surface highlights on the relief are largely fixed to the reference image rather than moving independently as pure metal reflections would.

The entire previous trophy was imported into live Blender as the working context. The reconstructed complete model was inspected at the front, both sides, back and an angled view. The editable scene is `emerald_trophy.blend`; its maps are packed inside. The selected USDZ is `mobile/emerald_trophy_badge_mobile.usdz`. Earlier models and scenes are preserved in `previous/` and their numbered candidate folders. `comparison.jpg` compares the reference with the native app view at equal outer diameters. `refinement.jpg` compares the previous and reconstructed app results. Candidate05 contains the app poses, four outline enlargements, carving detail, tracing mask, neutral measurement, material fit data and full turn recording.

The canonical complete gold shell, geometry, normals, UV sets, material graphs and texture bytes remain exactly preserved. Its name based runtime brushing, compact inner flashes and glint shield remain active. The green field uses the exact saved Emerald Archer color and roughness maps, metallic value and UV scale. Complete field coverage is checked by surface area, in addition to the existing app contract checks. The studio lighting, camera, floor, reflection, glow processing and Diamond gem shader are unchanged. No production app files were written.

The native simulator checks include front, both sides, back, full turn, reflection, and the outer outline at 4x. No new detached bright outer specks, broken shell outline or external bloom puffs were observed in those checks. Fine internal marks still reduce to a few phone pixels, and the smallest details are naturally softer than the large reference. The fixed app AgX transform also limits exact reproduction of some saturated gold hues. The wider approved rim makes the crest smaller relative to the complete coin than it is in the mockup. A thinner rim was offered for owner approval; it has not been applied without that answer.

Apple's ARKit USD checker and the local app contract validator pass. Units are meters, Y is up, transforms are identity, the shell is complete and closed, and the file contains no lights or cameras. No zero normals or degenerate triangles were found. Bounds are X and Y from minus 3.015 to 3.015, Z from minus 0.6853 to 0.3553. Floating placement remains size 0.96 and lift 0.56, with lowest world Y minus 2.455. `validation.json` records the final hash.

The selected file is 10,772,211 bytes and 154,724 triangles, using six combined relief meshes plus the shell. The size and triangle guidelines are exceeded to retain dense traced outlines, narrow stalks, continuous leaf and ribbon depth, closed side walls and matching sculpture on both faces. Simulator loading and rotation completed; physical device performance has not been measured. The private preview build succeeded with its existing FocusSessionManager unreachable code warning. No logic tests were added for this visual reconstruction. Geometry checks include a new complete field area check after a missing annular sector was found and repaired during development.

The separate preview remains Badge Studio on Badge Studio Preview, using `preview/ScreenTime.xcodeproj`, scheme `ScreenTime-UIOnly`, bundle `com.antonioperedo.badgestudio.preview`. The rebuilt trophy is left selected facing forward. No commits or branch changes were made.
