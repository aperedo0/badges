# Three completed badges

Ember Forge, Tidal Compass and Astral Navigator are complete, with editable Blender scenes, final models and native review evidence. Open Choose badge in Badge Studio Preview to switch between them. Astral Navigator is left open. Its menu selection persisted after stopping and reopening the app without a badge selection launch argument.

Each final was reviewed at the resting front, early turn, back, both sides and during a slow turn. Four times outline views and the floor reflection were inspected. All packages pass ARKit USD checks. Final file hashes match the reviewed candidates and installed preview models. The 32 earlier preview models remain byte identical, recorded in preservation_validation.json.

The work used live Blender and the separate native preview. The production app was not edited. These are distinctive reference frames; the approved shared twelve coin template remains preserved. Reference weathering and artwork use documented appearance fill, with measured real metal reflections during rotation. Tidal's pearl and wave are opaque smooth enamel relief, not transparent gemstones.

Astral uses real closed convex Sapphire crown and pavilion cuts. Nine front stones and nine matching reverse stones have actual runtime optics. Its new private material absorbs more light to match the deeper blue reference while preserving live reflections and refraction. Existing optical profiles and the approved Diamond retain their prior constants. See [Astral runtime notes](astral_navigator/RUNTIME.md) and [material patch](astral_navigator/runtime_material.patch). That runtime handling is required beyond the USDZ fallback. Individual facets and glints differ from a fixed illustration.

Astral's 88,556 triangles exceed the usual target to preserve fine scrollwork, scale markings, settings and gem cuts on both faces. Its package is 4.82 MB. Ember Forge is 2.83 MB and Tidal Compass is 2.63 MB. See the individual reports for source methods and validation details.

The run followed thirty minute work blocks and twenty minute breaks without an overall deadline. The recovery timer is stopped upon completion. Completed work must not restart on a delayed recovery message.
