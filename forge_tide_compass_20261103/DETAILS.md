# Reference details

Ember Forge: tall point up hexagonal case with dark forged outer bevel and copper inset trim; dark textured field; five curling red orange flame tongues behind the hammer; dark octagonal hammer head with bright chipped edges; straight handle with diagonal copper spiral and hexagonal cap; wide anvil top, twin curved cutouts, feet and lower framed ember diamond; four tapered flying sparks. Preserve deliberate weathering and ember texture from this reference.

Tidal Compass: slender silver circular frame around a deep teal faceted field; pale translucent turquoise crescent with sharp upper tip and a curling wave joining its lower sweep; separate dark teal and pale aqua lower wave layers; large silver compass with four long cardinal points and four shorter diagonal points, a thin circular inner guide, and four small markers touching the rim. Preserve smooth pearl surfaces without paper creases. The moon and waves are enamel or pearl surfaces rather than disconnected stones.

Astral Navigator: warm engraved gold outer rim; dark navy illustrated celestial chart with thin grids, constellation nodes and lines; a slim raised inner ring with dense alternating tick marks; four large cardinal points with blue inset faces, four diagonal gold points and gold tip markers; eight traced gold corner flourishes, paired crescents on left and right, small gold top and bottom stars and beads; central faceted blue cabochon, four cardinal diamond stones and four round diagonal stones, all independent closed convex cuts. Keep every visible small feature and the same detail on the rear.
