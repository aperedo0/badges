Sapphire development checkpoint

Only Sapphire Teardrop was worked on after the owner narrowed scope. Other badge geometry, materials and sources were untouched.

The gold setting now has separate real outer and inner bevels, plus smooth actual wall normals. The stone retains the measured six sided front table and broad crown planes. Its dense perimeter facets were reduced to24 real planes below the frame, removing the repeated stair pattern.

The actual USDZ passes the app contract and native convexity loader:3 meshes,4016 triangles,38 stone planes and279706 bytes. It has no reference appearance texture. Identity transforms, Y up, correct bounds, centered thickness.

Actual Blender renders cover front, early turn, both sides and back. An independent development review found no clear holes, speckle, broken outline or surface noise but scored reference design76 and judged it not ready for final native review: too many internal reflected divisions, grey left highlight, dark right facets, absent lower cyan, flat center and weak stepped gold border. These remain open.

Candidate19 tested a sloping rear table using unchanged optics. It did not improve the desired color and was rejected.

The separate simulator remains on its earlier installed candidate11. No preview app source, shared light, approved Diamond shader, production assets or app repository was changed during this scope. No final native acceptance or finish optimization claimed.
