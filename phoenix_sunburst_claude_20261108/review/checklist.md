PHOENIX SUNBURST CHECKLIST ((x%, y%) inside the badge box from the left and top)

OVERALL
* An ivory phoenix rising in front of a rayed sun. Top edge: 3 ray tips plus 2 upturned crescent wing tips; jagged feathered sides; bottom narrows to a cluster of downward flame tips.
* Width about 0.92 of height. Rays 0 to 14% of H; sun disc 14 to 61%; wings 11 to 73%; tail flames 62 to 100%.
* Widest at 33 to 35% height (outer red feather tips at 0% and 100% of W); second wide point at 58 to 60% (tips at 9% and 93%).
* Lowest point: central orange flame tip at 50% width, 100% height; ivory tail tip about 4% higher at 64% width; left flame tip at about 28%, 88%.
* Sun, crescents and wing outline nearly mirror symmetric; the phoenix is not (head faces right, S curved body, tail sweeps right).
* Cloisonne: every color field a recessed glossy enamel cell inside raised, rounded, mirror polished yellow gold walls; gold about a quarter of the visible area.
* Layers back to front: rays, sun disc and ring, crescents and red feathers, orange feathers, tail flames, the ivory phoenix in front.
* A gold side wall about 2 to 3% of W shows under the lower edges.

SUN
* Disc about 51% of W across, center (50%, 37%), top at 14%; lower third hidden behind body and wings.
* Disc enamel glossy, gently domed, bright lemon gold (#F7C800) upper center deepening to amber orange (#E09000) toward the bottom.
* Disc ring: polished gold band about 2% of W, rounded, fine inner step.
* Top ray: triangle at 50% width, tip at 0%, about 15% of W at the base and 14% of H tall, two flat facets split by a sharp vertical ridge (left light lemon, right deeper gold yellow), thin gold edge about 1% of W.
* Upper diagonal rays (2): tips at (26%, 7%) and (74%, 7%), about 85 to 90% the size of the top ray, same two facet yellow.
* Side rays (2): small triangles at about (19%, 24%) and (82%, 24%) wedged between the ring and the crescents.
* Black background gaps between the rays down to the ring.

WINGS (mirror the outline; the right shows more red)
* Crescents: tall sickle feathers with tips at (18%, 11%) and (82%, 12%) curling inward, about a third of H tall and up to 12% of W wide, deep crimson glossy enamel, maroon at the edges; outer gold border about 2% of W, inner edge about 2.5% and stepped, following the disc ring.
* Outer red feathers: tips form the leftmost (0%, 35%) and rightmost (100%, 33%) points, pointing up and out, crimson.
* Orange feathers: broad flame or leaf feathers, left upper tip (16%, 37%), left middle tip (17%, 52%), right upper tip (85%, 44%), right middle tip (88%, 56%), about 25% of W long and 8% wide, glossy orange (#FF7000), brighter yellow orange along the center, redder at the base.
* Lower red feathers: left tip (9%, 60%), right tip (93%, 58%); right lowest orange feather tip (74%, 73%).
* Large right red field from about 75 to 95% of W and 25 to 55% of H.
* Dark red or maroon enamel where the feathers meet the body.

PHOENIX (IVORY)
* One continuous S shaped ribbon from the crest (26% of H) to the tail tip (95%); ivory champagne (#E9DEC4) with a satin pearly sheen; gold outline about 1.5% of W; frontmost layer.
* Head small at about 57 to 65% width and 27 to 31% height, sharp beak pointing right with its tip at (65%, 29%), no eye.
* Crest sweeping back left: a red orange flame band between two thin ivory hooked plumes curling left (tips at about (34%, 27%) and (33%, 34%)), about 20% of W, all gold outlined.
* Neck about 5 to 6% of W thick, curving from the head down and left with yellow disc showing inside the S; lower left curl ends in a hook at (42%, 45%).
* Chest the widest ivory part, about 13% of W, bulging right to about 64% width at 52 to 56% height.
* Tail ribbon about 8 to 9% of W wide curving right in an S to a sharp tip at (64%, 95%).

TAIL FLAMES
* Left flame: red orange vermilion, tip pointing down and left at (28%, 88%), about 20% of H long.
* Center flame: the largest, amber orange (#F08A00) with a lighter yellow orange streak, from under the lower left wing to the lowest point (50%, 100%), about 30% of H long and up to 10% of W wide.
* Narrow crimson slivers between the center flame and the tail.

GOLD AND FINISHES
* Gold: mirror polished warm yellow gold, near white highlights and deep brown or olive reflections, half round profile.
* Gold widths: outer outline about 2% of W, inner walls 1 to 1.5%, disc ring 2%, ray edges 1%, crescent inner edges 2.5%.
* Red enamel crimson (#B8121E) to maroon at the cell edges, glossy deep candy look. Orange enamel bright orange to red orange at the bases. Ray facets flat yellow planes (#F5D640 and #E2AE10).
* Every feather, flame, ray and crescent tip ends in a sharp point; feather edges are flame like S curves.
* Back and sides not shown in the mockup.
